# Tips — future-block TAPOS

## Do

- Enforce delay ≥ 1.  
- Document expire/refund policy.  
- Prefer orng when the network has it.

## Don't

- Market TAPOS delay as equivalent to threshold RNG.  
- Resolve twice.  
- Accept unstructured memo seeds without length/hex checks.
